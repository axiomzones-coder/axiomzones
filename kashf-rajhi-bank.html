Why Your Bank Loan Costs More Than You Think

📊 APR vs IRR

Banks advertise APR calculated on the original balance. IRR calculates the true cost accounting for balance reduction. The gap is always significant.

🎭 Beautification Score

Our proprietary metric (0-100) measures how much the bank hides. Score 70+ means significant hidden costs you were not told about.

💰 Hidden Fees

Administrative fees, insurance, and service charges are often added to the loan and accrue interest — increasing the true cost silently.
