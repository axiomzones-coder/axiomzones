FAQ

Common Questions

Is Axiom Zones really free to start?▼
Yes — permanently. Every platform has a free tier that never expires and requires no credit card. The free tiers are genuinely useful, not crippled demos. We believe in earning your subscription through demonstrated value, not trapping you with paywalls.
One account works across all platforms?▼
Yes. Create one Axiom Zones account and it grants access to every platform you subscribe to — on web, iOS, Android, Windows, and macOS. Your subscriptions travel with you across all devices seamlessly.
How does the Arab World pricing work?▼
We apply a 50% discount for users from Egypt, Morocco, Jordan, Tunisia, Algeria, Libya, Iraq, Syria, Yemen, Sudan, and Lebanon. This is detected automatically by your IP and currency selection. The goal is accessibility — the Arab world deserves professional tools at fair local prices.
Is my financial data stored or shared?▼
No. All calculations run in your browser. Numbers are never sent to our servers. When you share a link, the data is encoded in the URL itself — we never see it. Your account data (email, subscription status) is stored with full encryption via Supabase.
What is the Lifetime Deal — will it really close?▼
The Lifetime Deal ($399, one-time) gives Pro All-Access to every current and future platform, forever. It closes permanently after 100 accounts — not as a marketing tactic, but because we can only sustainably support a limited number of lifetime accounts. When it's gone, it's gone permanently.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?▼
Yes. Cancel from your dashboard in one click with no questions asked. You keep access until the end of your billing period, then drop to the free tier automatically. No hidden fees, no retention emails, no dark patterns.
When will mobile apps launch?▼
All platforms are PWA-ready now — install them from your browser on any device and they work like native apps with offline support. Dedicated App Store and Play Store apps are in development, expected Q2 2025.
Do you offer refunds?▼
Yes. 14-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans, no questions asked. If Axiom Zones doesn't deliver clear value in your first two weeks, contact us and we refund immediately. The Lifetime Deal also has a 14-day refund window.
