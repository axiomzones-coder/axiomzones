Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 1, 2024 · Last Updated: March 1, 2024 · Axiom Zones · axiomzones.com

Summary: We collect only what we need, we never sell your data, and you can delete your account at any time. This policy explains everything in plain language.

1. Who We Are

Axiom Zones ("we", "our", "us") operates axiomzones.com and all sub-platforms including Prism Suite, Kashf, UniUber, Master 365, Mizan Pro, Kyanat, ProSuite 365, and Tripedia. We are a software company providing financial analysis, business intelligence, and SaaS tools globally.

Contact: privacy@axiomzones.com

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account data: Name, email address, password (encrypted)
  • Profile data: Country, phone number (optional)
  • Payment data: Processed entirely by Stripe — we never see or store your card details
  • Financial inputs: Loan amounts and figures you enter into our calculators (stored locally in your browser, not on our servers, unless you create an account)
  • Communications: Messages you send to our support team

Information Collected Automatically

  • Browser type, device type, operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent (via Google Analytics)
  • IP address and approximate location (country/city level only)
  • Referral source (how you found us)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate and improve our platforms
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To send account-related emails (welcome, invoices, alerts)
  • To send marketing emails only if you opted in
  • To detect and prevent fraud or misuse
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To generate anonymized, aggregate usage statistics

4. Data Sharing

We never sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Stripe — payment processing (PCI-DSS compliant)
  • Supabase — database and authentication hosting
  • Vercel — website hosting and deployment
  • Google Analytics — anonymized usage statistics
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — AI-powered translation and chat features (no personal data is sent to AI systems)
  • Legal authorities — only when required by law

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers provided by Supabase (hosted on AWS). We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Encrypted passwords (bcrypt hashing)
  • Row-level security (RLS) in our database
  • Regular security audits

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction — Update inaccurate information
  • Deletion — Delete your account and all associated data
  • Portability — Export your data in a standard format
  • Opt-out — Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time
  • Objection — Object to certain types of processing

To exercise these rights, email privacy@axiomzones.com or use the Settings page in your dashboard.

7. Cookies

We use cookies for:

  • Essential cookies: Session management, authentication
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics (you can opt out)
  • Preference cookies: Language and theme settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect platform functionality.

8. Children's Privacy

Our platforms are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us immediately.

9. International Users

We operate globally. If you are in the European Union (EU), your data is processed in accordance with GDPR. If you are in the United Kingdom, your data is processed in accordance with UK GDPR. If you are in California, you have additional rights under CCPA.

10. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., financial records for 7 years).

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of significant changes via email. The "Last Updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our platforms after changes constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact Us

Questions about your privacy?

Email: privacy@axiomzones.com

Response time: Within 48 hours
